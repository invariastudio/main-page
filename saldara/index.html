Privacy boundary

Local-first, stated precisely.

What stays local

  • The financial ledger and its descriptions, names, amounts, budgets, reports, and audit history.
  • Core calculations and deterministic analysis.
  • Reminder storage, protected by Android Keystore encryption.
  • Backups, until you explicitly save an encrypted copy to a destination you choose.

When the network is used

  • An optional USD/BRL PTAX date request to Banco Central do Brasil.
  • Google Play purchase, restore, and narrowly scoped Premium verification.
  • A user-initiated visit to these public privacy or support pages.

No ledger content is added to those requests. See the complete privacy policy for the exact fields and parties.

Free and Premium

Trust features stay free.

Premium is planned as a one-time Google Play purchase. Final price and availability will be shown by Google Play when the product is active.

Free foundation

The manual ledger, accounts and cards, recurring entries, installments, budgets, reconciliation, reports, encrypted storage, encrypted backup, export, app lock, audit history, privacy controls, and accessibility remain available without Premium.

Lifetime Premium

The first Premium release is designed to unlock OFX and mapped CSV statement import, deterministic category rules, and on-device category suggestions with explicit evidence.

Premium does not turn existing financial records into inaccessible data if an entitlement cannot be verified.

O que o Saldara faz

Registra contas, carteiras, cartões, despesas, receitas, transferências, estornos, recorrências e parcelas. Ajuda a planejar orçamentos, conciliar saldos e entender relatórios calculados no aparelho.

O que o Saldara não é

O Saldara não é banco, serviço de pagamento, credor, corretora nem consultoria financeira. Ele não conecta contas bancárias automaticamente e não usa IA generativa para analisar seu histórico.

Availability

Saldara is not currently offered for public download. This page will link to the official Google Play listing when the release is available. Do not install files claiming to be Saldara from unofficial sources.
