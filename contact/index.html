What to include

Enough context, without sensitive records.

Studio questions

Include a clear subject and the product or page involved. The studio can answer questions about public information, product availability, privacy, accessibility, and documented behavior.

Saldara support

For an app problem, include the Saldara version, Android version, expected result, actual result, and reproduction steps. The Saldara support page has troubleshooting guidance.

Do not email financial or secret data

Do not send backup passwords, encryption keys, purchase tokens, real account identifiers, transaction descriptions, screenshots with real balances, or monetary amounts. Invaria Studio does not need those details to begin investigating a report.
