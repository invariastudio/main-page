How we work

Standards that can be checked.

Invaria Studio is new. These principles describe how its products and public claims should be evaluated.

Working principles

Small promises, kept precisely.

01

Describe the real product

Clearly distinguish what exists now, what is being prepared, and what is only a future possibility. Do not invent reviews, customer counts, awards, certifications, or partnerships.

02

Collect less

Do not add accounts, analytics, advertising technology, telemetry, or third-party services without a concrete product need and an accurate public explanation.

03

Make boundaries visible

Explain where data lives, when a network is used, what a purchase unlocks, what can be recovered, and what the product does not provide.

04

Prefer understandable systems

Use deterministic behavior where practical, keep important actions reviewable, and make failures explicit instead of hiding uncertainty behind confident language.

Public accountability

If a product or page does not follow these principles, report the specific inconsistency through the contact page.
